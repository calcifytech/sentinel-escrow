[1:00 - ARCHITECTURE]
(show assets/architecture_diagram.png)
Let's look at the system architecture. First, any contract or release request goes through our Security Checkpoint node. This node intercepts raw inputs, redacts sensitive private data, and filters out prompt injection attacks. If it passes, the Orchestrator Agent takes over. The orchestrator coordinates the audit by delegating tasks to two specialized sub-agents: the Contract Compliance Auditor and the Escrow Funding Agent. These sub-agents query our local FastMCP server tools to verify account balances and check payee compliance lists. Once the sub-agents report back, the orchestrator recommends releasing or holding the funds. Finally, the workflow pauses at a Human Approval Checkpoint, requiring a manager's signature before executing the payment.
